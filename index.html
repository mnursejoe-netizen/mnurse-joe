Bring or send

  • Your specific question, or the decision the research should inform
  • The paper(s), links or DOIs, or your draft
  • Your audience (clinical staff, students, journal) and any required format

What a review covers

  • Study design and quality (bias, sample, methods, statistics)
  • Strength of the evidence and how it applies to practice
  • Plain-language summary of findings and limits
  • Suggested next steps or further reading
