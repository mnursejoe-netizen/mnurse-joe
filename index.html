Guide: preparing for your session

Bring or send

  • Your specific question or the decision the research should inform
  • The paper(s), links or DOIs, or your draft
  • Your audience (clinical staff, students, patients, a journal) and any required format
  • Your deadline

What a review covers

  • Study design and quality (bias, sample, methods, statistics)
  • How strong the evidence is and how well it applies to practice
  • Plain-language summary of findings and limits
  • Suggestions for further reading or next steps

Policies

  • Rescheduling: at least 24 hours' notice.
  • Scope: educational and research review only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. For personal health concerns, contact your clinician.
  • Privacy: do not send identifiable patient information. De-identify all materials.
